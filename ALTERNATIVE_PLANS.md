### The Starving Crowd

**The dean walks into the department chair's office**: "Justify your program's existence with data, or we're merging you into General Arts next year."

The music department chair has NO tool to answer this. They have:
- Anecdotal evidence ("our students love it")

**This is an expensive problem.** Losing a university music program means:

**They would pay $25K-100K/yr for a tool that prevents this.** That's 1-2% of what program closure costs them.

## Plan C: AI Sync Intelligence Platform — "SyncIQ"

**One-liner**: "Find the perfect track in 60 seconds, not 6 hours. Close more sync deals, faster."

---

### The Starving Crowd

**Music supervisors and sync licensing agencies are drowning in music.**

The numbers:
- **150,000 songs uploaded daily** to streaming platforms (2025)
- **$650M sync licensing market** (2024), growing 7.4%/yr
- **70% of sync deals** go through music libraries
- By 2026, **65% of supervisors** are expected to use AI tools for music search
- A single sync placement: **$5,000-25,000** for TV, up to **$500K+** for major film/ads
- Cross-border sync deals up **30% since 2025**
- Music libraries power **80% of placements** (MIDiA Research, 2026)

**The problem**: A music supervisor gets a brief — "We need an upbeat, nostalgic synth-pop track with female vocals for a car commercial, 30 seconds, needs to feel like driving at sunset."

Today they:
1. Search 5-10 music libraries manually (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, etc.)
2. Listen to hundreds of tracks, each for 10-30 seconds
3. Shortlist 15-20 candidates
4. Create a presentation for the client
5. Negotiate licensing for the selected track

**This process takes 4-8 hours per placement.** A busy supervisor handles 10-20 briefs/month. That's 40-160 hours/month just SEARCHING for music.

And they're STILL missing gems because no human can listen to 150,000 new tracks per day. Placements are being left on the table. Revenue is being lost.

**A tool that cuts search time by 80% directly increases their revenue capacity.**

### The Product

SyncIQ is a B2B platform for music supervisors, sync agencies, and production music libraries that uses AI to analyze, categorize, and match music to briefs instantly.

#### Core Features

**1. Brief-to-Match Engine (The Core)**
Paste or type a creative brief → AI returns ranked matches from connected catalogs:

```
Brief: "Upbeat nostalgic synth-pop, female vocals, sunset driving
       feeling, 30 seconds for car commercial, similar energy to
       'Blinding Lights' but more feminine and warm"

    Synth-pop, Female vocals, 128 BPM, Key: Ab major
    Mood: Nostalgic, Uplifting, Warm
    Why: Similar BPM and key to reference. Warm analog synths with
    female vocals. Chorus hits at 0:12 — perfect for 30s edit.
    → [Preview] [License Info] [Add to Shortlist]

#2  "Neon Dreams" — Glass Canyon          Match: 91%
    Synthwave, Female vocals, 132 BPM, Key: F minor
    Mood: Nostalgic, Energetic, Dreamy
    Why: Driving rhythm section with retro synth palette. Slightly
    more melancholy than reference but strong sunset imagery.
    → [Preview] [License Info] [Add to Shortlist]

#3  "Coastline" — Marine Blue            Match: 88%
    ...
```

**2. Catalog Intelligence**
For music libraries — AI analyzes and enriches your entire catalog:
- Auto-tag: mood, genre, sub-genre, instrumentation, tempo, key, energy, vocal type, lyrical themes
- Quality scoring: production quality rating, commercial viability score
- Gap analysis: "Your catalog is strong in upbeat pop but weak in ambient electronic — that's a growing sync category"
- Trend matching: "These 47 tracks in your catalog match the sound profile trending in automotive ads right now"

**3. Supervisor Dashboard**
- Manage all briefs in one place
- Track pitch-to-placement conversion rates
- Client preference learning: "This client always picks tracks in minor keys with 120-130 BPM"
- One-click shortlist presentations with embedded previews

**4. Cross-Catalog Search**
- Connect multiple library accounts (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, etc.)
- Search across ALL connected catalogs simultaneously
- Deduplicate and compare licensing terms

**5. Trend Intelligence**
- "Sync placements in automotive ads are 40% more likely to use retro-synth sounds this quarter"
- "Female-vocal indie folk is trending in lifestyle brand ads — up 60% YoY"
- Based on aggregated placement data and music analysis

### Technical Architecture

```
Frontend:   Next.js 15 + React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind
Audio:      Essentia (audio feature extraction: mood, tempo, key, energy)
            Basic Pitch (melodic content)
            Demucs (stem separation for deeper analysis)
Embeddings: Custom audio embeddings for similarity search
            (Clap/Laion or fine-tuned model)
Search:     Vector database (Pinecone/Qdrant) for embedding similarity
            + structured metadata filtering
AI Layer:   Claude API — brief interpretation, match explanation,
            trend analysis, catalog gap reports
Database:   Supabase (users, briefs, placements, analytics)
Storage:    S3/Cloudflare R2 (audio file references, analysis cache)
```

**Key technical insight**: The hard part isn't the AI — it's the **catalog integrations** and **audio embeddings pipeline**. Start with a single library (or user-uploaded catalogs) to validate, then expand integrations.
